## 1. The premise that was wrong

An earlier platform scan asserted: *"The European Accessibility Act has applied
since June 2025. A published accessibility statement is a legal requirement for
a service of this kind operating from Portugal."*

Both halves of the second sentence look incorrect, on two independent grounds.
The dates in the first sentence are right.

**Ground one, scope.** DL 82/2022 art. 2.º n.º 3 is a closed list of the
services the decree-law reaches: electronic communications (a); access to
audiovisual media services (b); named elements of air, bus, rail, maritime and
inland-waterway passenger transport (c); interactive self-service terminals for
urban, suburban and regional transport (d); consumer banking and financial
services (e); e-books and their dedicated software (f); e-commerce services (g).
Art. 2.º n.º 4 adds 112 emergency call handling. A non-commercial community
platform is on none of those lists. The only alínea it could ever fall under is
(g), and QueerPulse has no payment processor of any kind and takes no money from
anyone.

**Ground two, the microenterprise exemption.** DL 82/2022 art. 2.º n.º 5 al. b),
verbatim: *"O presente decreto-lei não se aplica: ... b) Às microempresas que
prestam os serviços previstos no n.º 3."* No conditions, no partial application.
The definition comes from art. 3.º, which imports the Directive's definitions
wholesale; Directive art. 3(23) defines a microenterprise as one employing fewer
than 10 persons with an annual turnover **or** annual balance sheet total not
exceeding EUR 2 million.

**The statement duty people usually mean is a different law.** The obligation to
publish an *accessibility statement* in the familiar sense comes from the Web
Accessibility Directive, (EU) 2016/2102, transposed as Decreto-Lei n.º 83/2018,
which binds public sector bodies. DL 82/2022 art. 2.º n.º 6 al. f) expressly
preserves it as a separate regime. QueerPulse is not a public sector body.

DL 82/2022's own statement-like duty is narrower: art. 13.º n.º 2 al. b) requires
an in-scope service provider to *"Explicar de que forma os serviços cumprem os
requisitos de acessibilidade aplicáveis, devendo tais informações ser
disponibilizadas ao público por escrito e oralmente, de maneira acessível a
pessoas com deficiência, e mantidas enquanto o serviço estiver disponível."*
Note "e oralmente": even in scope, this is not satisfied by a web page alone.

**Dates, for the record.** DL 82/2022 entered into force the day after
publication, so 7 December 2022 (art. 40.º). It produces effects for services
provided from 28 June 2025 (art. 39.º n.º 1); the 112 obligations land 28 June
2027 (art. 39.º n.º 2); transitional cover for pre-existing installations and
contracts runs to 28 June 2030 (art. 38.º).

## 2. One correction to the research this document came from

The brief that commissioned this work said ASAE is the Article 28 regulator for
e-commerce. **It is not.** DL 82/2022 art. 28.º n.º 1 al. a) gives ANACOM *"os
serviços identificados nas alíneas a) e g) do n.º 3 e ao previsto no n.º 4 do
artigo 2.º"*. Alínea a) of n.º 3 is electronic communications services, alínea
g) is **e-commerce services**, and n.º 4 is 112 handling. ASAE's remit under art. 28.º n.º 1 al. g) is
products: IT equipment that does not work by radio, operating systems, and
several classes of self-service terminal. ASAE also verifies CE marking and the
EU declaration (art. 28.º n.º 2 al. a)).

So if QueerPulse ever does become an e-commerce service, its regulator is
**ANACOM**. The public page says ANACOM. Do not "correct" it back to ASAE.

The rest of art. 28.º n.º 1, for completeness: ERC for audiovisual media access
(b); the Autoridade da Mobilidade e dos Transportes and ANAC for passenger
transport (c); the Instituto da Mobilidade e dos Transportes, I. P. for transport
self-service terminals (d); Banco de Portugal for credit, payment services,
payment accounts and e-money (e); CMVM for investment services (f); municipalities
for ATMs (h); the Inspeção-Geral das Atividades Culturais for e-books (i).

## 3. INR is now IDiPD

DL 82/2022 art. 36.º names *"o INR, I. P."* as the body responsible for
following and monitoring the decree-law, and art. 36.º n.º 2 al. a) makes it the
body that forwards complaints sent to it directly on to the art. 28.º regulator
that can act on them.

That body has been renamed. DL 60/2026 art. 1.º al. a) restructures the
Instituto Nacional para a Reabilitação, I. P. (INR, I. P.) so that it *"passa a
denominar-se Instituto para os Direitos das Pessoas com Deficiência, I. P.
(IDiPD, I. P.)"*, and art. 2.º provides that *"As referências ao INR, I. P., ou à
EMPA, constantes de diplomas legais e regulamentares, actos, contratos e outros
instrumentos normativos consideram-se feitas ao IDiPD, I. P."* The same
decree-law extinguishes the Estrutura de Missão para a Promoção das
Acessibilidades (EMPA) and folds its competences in. It was published 24
February 2026 and entered into force on the first day of the following month
(art. 5.º), so 1 March 2026.

Consequence: the art. 36.º reference in DL 82/2022 now reads as IDiPD, and any
document that still says INR is citing a renamed body. **Anything written here
after 1 March 2026 says IDiPD.**

We publish no phone number, postal address or email for IDiPD, because nobody
here has verified one. The public page links to `idipd.mtsss.gov.pt` and lets the
institute own its own contact details.

## 4. What would change the answer

Paid ticketing on gatherings is an open product question. It is the single
change most likely to move QueerPulse into scope, because selling a ticket is
what would make it plausible to call the platform a *serviço de comércio
eletrónico* under art. 2.º n.º 3 al. g).

If that ships, three things have to be re-checked in order, and none of them is
answerable today:

1. **Is it e-commerce under al. g)?** Selling our own tickets, taking a cut of a
   host's sale, and merely embedding someone else's checkout are not obviously
   the same answer.
2. **Does the art. 2.º n.º 5 al. b) microenterprise exemption still hold?** Both
   limbs, headcount and money, against whatever legal entity is doing the
   selling.
3. **If in scope and not exempt, art. 13.º n.º 2 al. b) attaches**, and it asks
   for the explanation *in writing and orally*. The published statement covers
   the writing. Nothing covers the oral channel today, and QueerPulse has no
   telephone line and no email
   (`src/account/account.constants.ts:29-35`, and see
   [`no-email-at-launch.md`](no-email-at-launch.md)). That is a real gap to design for, and it
   needs a channel before it needs a paragraph.

Treat this section as a trigger to go and read the law again. It is not a
prediction of what the answer will be.

## 5. Still needs a lawyer (internal only)

None of the following is settled, and none of it appears on the public page.
The public page states the reading and says plainly that no lawyer has reviewed
it.

1. **Is QueerPulse an "undertaking" / "operador económico" at all?** DL 82/2022
   speaks throughout of *operadores económicos* and *prestadores de serviços*.
   The microenterprise test in Directive art. 3(23) is framed in terms of an
   *enterprise* with employees and turnover. A non-commercial platform run
   without revenue may sit outside that vocabulary entirely, which would make
   the scope argument in §1 the only one that matters and the exemption
   argument redundant. Nobody here can say which.
2. **Is the microenterprise test met?** It needs the actual legal entity behind
   QueerPulse, its headcount, and its turnover or balance sheet total. That is a
   question of fact about a company, and this repository is not evidence of it.
3. **Does any Portuguese disability-discrimination law outside the EAA create a
   duty regardless of EAA scope?** The one to look at is **Lei n.º 46/2006, de 28
   de agosto** (prevention and prohibition of discrimination on grounds of
   disability). DL 82/2022 art. 2.º n.º 6 al. d) expressly says the decree-law
   *"não prejudica a aplicação"* of Lei 46/2006, so being out of EAA scope
   settles nothing about it. Lei n.º 38/2004 (the framework law on the rights of
   disabled people) and Decreto-Lei n.º 163/2006 (built-environment
   accessibility, also preserved by art. 2.º n.º 6 al. c)) are the next two to
   read.
4. **Which entity is the service provider for a gathering's ticket?** Bears on
   §4 item 1 and on who the regulated party would be.

Until a lawyer answers 1 to 3, the public page's framing must stay as it is: a
stated reading, attributed to the people who run the platform, carrying the
words "Not legal advice".
